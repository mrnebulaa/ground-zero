# Contributing

We welcome code, accessibility improvements, documentation, source-methodology critiques, and reproducible bug reports from people of different viewpoints.

1. Open an issue describing the problem or proposal before major changes.
2. Keep pull requests focused; explain motivation, tests, and privacy/security impact.
3. Link evidence for factual assertions. Never include secrets, personal records, or copyrighted documents without permission.
4. Be respectful; criticize arguments and implementation, not identities.
5. Maintainers review contributions for correctness, security, accessibility, and consistency with the charter—not political alignment.
6. By contributing, you agree that your contribution is distributed under AGPL-3.0-only.

Security vulnerabilities: do not publish exploit details in public issues; see SECURITY.md.
