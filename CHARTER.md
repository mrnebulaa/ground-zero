# Ground Zero Project Charter

## Mission

Help people independently verify factual claims, investigate evidence, discuss constructively, and hold powerful institutions accountable. Ground Zero is open source and nonpartisan. Its labels are provisional assessments, never guarantees of truth.

## Principles

1. Evidence over popularity: inspect source quality, relevance, independence, and uncertainty rather than counting votes.
2. Transparency over manipulation: expose reasoning, corrections, and material limitations.
3. Free expression with protection against harassment: welcome disagreement; prohibit threats, doxxing, impersonation, and targeted abuse.
4. Political neutrality in governance: apply published rules consistently regardless of party, identity, or viewpoint.
5. Public corrections and accountable moderation: explain public decisions and provide meaningful appeals while protecting sensitive information.
6. No behavioral profiling or engagement-maximizing recommendation algorithms.
7. Open-source code and community participation under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
8. Human oversight of AI: label AI-generated material and require accountable human review. AI must not fabricate citations or independently adjudicate truth.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Separate factual assertions, interpretations, opinions, and unknowns. A linked source is not automatically reliable or supporting evidence. Prefer original sources; explain authorship, dates, scope, limitations, and potential conflicts. Contributors' support/challenge/context labels are assessments, not verified source conclusions.

Unreviewed means no completed assessment. Under Review means examination is underway. Supported means the reviewed evidence supports the statement within its stated scope. Disputed means significant credible disagreement remains. Refuted means the reviewed evidence contradicts the statement. Insufficient Evidence means the available material cannot sustain a conclusion. None of these labels makes a claim infallible. Opinion and interpretation must not be presented as objective fact merely because a label is applied.

## Governance and accountability

Publish policy changes, funding conflicts, reviewer conflicts, and aggregate moderation reports before scaling. Moderators must give an understandable public reason and appeal route; private reports and investigation notes must stay private. Reviewers must consider dissenting evidence and disclose limitations. Establish independent oversight and operational appeals before public participation.

## Privacy and correction

Collect minimal data. Do not publish personal or sensitive records in claims. Keep ordinary correction histories public. Before launch, establish a narrowly authorized privacy/redaction process that removes harmful personal material while recording a public explanation without repeating it. No immutable-history promise overrides privacy protection.
