# Ground Zero Project Charter — v0.1

## Mission
Help people independently evaluate public-interest claims using inspectable evidence, transparent corrections, and constructive disagreement.

## Editorial independence
Ground Zero does not endorse political parties or treat any institution, founder, moderator, majority vote, or AI system as an unquestionable authority.

## Evidence standards
Claims should include primary sources where available, publication dates, scope, and material uncertainty. Distinguish direct evidence from inference. A claim's status is provisional and may change with new evidence. Keep public correction and appeal histories, while protecting private information.

## Community
Allow good-faith disagreement. Prohibit threats, harassment, doxxing, impersonation, coordinated manipulation, and spam. Apply published rules consistently and provide meaningful appeals.

## AI policy
AI may assist research and moderation triage, but must not independently adjudicate truth or silently fabricate citations. Clearly label AI-generated content and allow human review.

## Governance
Publish moderation policies and aggregate transparency reports. Establish independent oversight before scaling. Financial conflicts and major policy changes should be disclosed.

## Privacy
Collect minimal personal data, use secure defaults, and do not build individual political persuasion profiles.
